The firm

Reindaxia Engineering & Advising

Engineering and consulting firm founded in 2021 in La Soukra, Tunisia. We work for owners, contractors and industrial operators in energy, oil and gas, industry and infrastructure, on their sites and at their suppliers abroad.

Assignments are led by senior engineers from EPC projects, quality, inspection and audit. Each assignment has a written scope, named deliverables and a report in which every finding refers to evidence.

Second party

Inspections and audits on behalf of the buyer or owner. No certification and no statutory inspection, which belong to accredited or approved bodies.

Independence

Never both parties on one project. Conflicts of interest declared before we start.

Confidentiality

Documents and findings stay with the client. Non-disclosure agreement signed on request.
