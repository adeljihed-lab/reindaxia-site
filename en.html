What we do

Services

Three complementary fields, from the factory to taking over. Each assignment has a written scope, named deliverables and a fixed price.

Quality and acceptancefrom manufacturing to commissioning

Quality support and expertise

Owner’s quality representative, review and approval of contractor quality plans and inspection and test plans, quality surveillance, NCR and KPI follow-up, QA/QC staff secondment.

Equipment inspection and acceptance

On the buyer’s behalf (second party): vendor assessment, manufacturing surveillance, factory acceptance test (FAT) witnessing, final and pre-shipment inspection with release note, expediting, site receiving and SAT.

Mechanical completion and commissioning support

Systemisation, punch-list management, verification of mechanical completion dossiers, commissioning supervision, handover dossiers, taking-over and warranty support.

HSE and environmentpeople, plant and environment

HSE and environment

Project HSE plans, contractor environmental and social management plans (C-ESMP) and site-specific plans, risk assessments and job safety analyses, explosive atmospheres (IEC 60079), implementation monitoring and E&S reporting for lender-financed projects.

Systems, audits and contractsmanagement systems, audits, project control, contracts

Management system implementation

Setting up, integrating and certifying ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 management systems: gap assessment, processes, documentation, training, internal audits, management review, certification support and transition to ISO 9001:2026.

Audits

Supplier and subcontractor (second-party) audits, project quality audits, internal audits on your behalf, ISO 9001:2026 gap assessment, ISO 9001 implementation support.

Project and contract management

Owner’s project management support, project controls (schedule, cost, earned value, risk), procurement support for buyers, contract administration under FIDIC conditions, claims and dispute avoidance.

Training

In-company training

ISO 9001:2026 internal auditor; quality on construction projects; mechanical completion, commissioning and taking-over; industrial and EPC project management; Tunisian public procurement; construction contracts based on FIDIC conditions (independent course); production planning and scheduling; Lean and problem solving. Delivered in French or English.

Independence. Reindaxia does not issue certificates or perform statutory inspections; these belong to accredited or approved bodies. We disclose any conflict of interest before an assignment and never work for two parties on the same project.
